# Extended Technology Stats - Levels 11-30

This document shows the **actual game effects** (damage, defense, speed, energy, etc.) of technologies beyond level 10.

## Scaling Methodology

Based on analysis of existing components, here's how stats scale:

- **Weapons:** +1 damage per Mk level (approximately)
- **Shields/Armor:** +1 defense per Mk level
- **Engines:** +1 FTL speed per Mk level (approximately)
- **Power Sources:** +1 energy output per Mk level
- **Electronics:** Gradual improvements (cloaking %, scanner range, etc.)
- **Energy Requirements:** Reduced by 1 every 2 levels beyond 10 (as per requirements)

## COMPARISON: Level 10 vs Level 30

### Example: Destroyer-Class Ship

**Level 10 Configuration:**
- Antimatter beam Mk2: 10 damage, 4 energy
- Shield Mk10: 10 defense, 3 energy
- Warp drive Mk8: 13 FTL speed, 3 energy
- Antimatter source Mk2: 13 energy output
- **Total Energy:** 13 output - 10 requirement = **+3 energy**

**Level 30 Configuration:**
- Antimatter beam Mk21: 30 damage, 1 energy
- Shield Mk30: 30 defense, 1 energy  
- Warp drive Mk21: 33 FTL speed, 1 energy
- Antimatter source Mk21: 33 energy output
- **Total Energy:** 33 output - 3 requirement = **+30 energy**

**Result:** Level 30 ship has:
- **3x damage** (30 vs 10)
- **3x defense** (30 vs 10)
- **2.5x speed** (33 vs 13 FTL)
- **10x energy surplus** (30 vs 3)

### Example: Capital Ship

**Level 10 Capital Ship:**
- No free energy
- Standard hull points

**Level 30 Dreadnought:**
- **+40 free energy** (from hull)
- **+200% hull points** (3x durability)
- Can mount more weapons/defenses
- Much harder to destroy

## STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS

### Early Extended Levels (11-15)
- **Moderate improvements** over level 10
- **Reduced energy costs** make ships more efficient
- **Worth researching** for longer games (600+ star years)

### Mid Extended Levels (16-20)
- **Significant power increase**
- **Free energy from hulls** enables new ship designs
- **Essential** for competitive late-game play

### High Extended Levels (21-30)
- **Massive power scaling**
- **Game-changing** technologies
- **Dyson spheres** provide planet-scale energy
- **Dreadnoughts** dominate space combat

### Energy Efficiency Revolution

One of the key features: **components use less energy at higher levels**

- Level 10 weapon: 4 energy
- Level 20 weapon: 2 energy  
- Level 30 weapon: 1 energy

This means ships can mount **more weapons** or **stronger defenses** without needing massive power sources!

## RECOMMENDED USAGE

- **Standard Games (200-400 star years):** Max level 10 (default)
- **Long Games (600-800 star years):** Max level 15-20
- **Epic Games (1000+ star years):** Max level 25-30

The extended technology system ensures that research remains meaningful throughout even the longest games, providing continuous progression and strategic depth!
