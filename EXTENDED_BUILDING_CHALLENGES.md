# Extended Technology Building Challenges - Design Ideas

This document provides creative challenge ideas for buildings unlocked by extended technology levels (11+). These challenges add strategic depth and prevent buildings from being "free power-ups."

---

## CHALLENGE CATEGORIES

### 1. **Resource Requirements**

#### High Initial Costs
- **Advanced Research Lab (Level 11):**
  - Production Cost: 80-100 (vs 20-40 for regular labs)
  - Metal Cost: 60-80 (vs 10-20 for regular labs)
  - **Challenge:** Requires significant investment, delaying other projects

- **Quantum Factory (Level 12):**
  - Production Cost: 120-150
  - Metal Cost: 100-120
  - **Challenge:** Massive upfront investment

- **Nano Factory (Level 15):**
  - Production Cost: 200-250
  - Metal Cost: 150-200
  - **Challenge:** Requires multiple turns of focused production

- **Dyson Sphere Component (Level 16+):**
  - Production Cost: 300-500 (per component!)
  - Metal Cost: 200-300
  - **Challenge:** Requires 3-5 components to complete = 900-2500 total production!

#### Rare Material Requirements
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires "Exotic Matter" (found only in neutron star systems)
- **Nano Factory:** Requires "Rare Earth Elements" (found only on certain planet types)
- **Dyson Sphere:** Requires "Solar Collectors" (special component, can only be built in space)

**Implementation:** Add new resource type or require specific tech/component to be researched first.

---

### 2. **Planet Requirements**

#### Minimum Planet Size
- **Advanced Research Lab:** Requires planet size 12+ (large planets only)
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires planet size 14+ (very large planets)
- **Nano Factory:** Requires planet size 16+ (huge planets)
- **Dyson Sphere:** Requires planet size 18+ (maximum size planets)

**Challenge:** Forces players to prioritize large planets for advanced buildings.

#### Minimum Population
- **Advanced Research Lab:** Requires 8+ population (needs large workforce)
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires 10+ population (needs specialized workers)
- **Nano Factory:** Requires 12+ population (needs massive workforce)
- **Dyson Sphere:** Requires 15+ population (needs planet-scale workforce)

**Challenge:** Can't build on new colonies - must develop planet first.

#### Planet Type Restrictions
- **Quantum Factory:** Only on Terrestrial/Desert planets (needs stable surface)
- **Nano Factory:** Only on Terrestrial/Volcanic planets (needs geological stability)
- **Dyson Sphere:** Only on Terrestrial planets (needs solid surface for anchor points)

**Challenge:** Limits where buildings can be constructed.

---

### 3. **Maintenance & Ongoing Costs**

#### High Maintenance Costs
- **Advanced Research Lab:**
  - Maintenance: 2-3 credits per turn
  - **Challenge:** Ongoing cost reduces credit income

- **Quantum Factory:**
  - Maintenance: 4-5 credits per turn
  - **Challenge:** Significant ongoing expense

- **Nano Factory:**
  - Maintenance: 6-8 credits per turn
  - **Challenge:** Requires strong economy to support

- **Dyson Sphere Component:**
  - Maintenance: 10-15 credits per turn (per component!)
  - **Challenge:** Massive ongoing cost, but provides energy income

#### Energy Requirements
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires 5 energy from planet (needs massive power)
- **Nano Factory:** Requires 10 energy from planet (needs extreme power)
- **Dyson Sphere:** Provides energy, but initial construction requires 20 energy

**Challenge:** Must have sufficient power infrastructure before building.

#### Population Maintenance
- **Advanced Research Lab:** Requires 1 population to operate (reduces workforce)
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires 2 population to operate
- **Nano Factory:** Requires 3 population to operate

**Challenge:** Reduces available workforce for other tasks.

---

### 4. **Prerequisites & Dependencies**

#### Building Prerequisites
- **Advanced Research Lab:** Requires "Research Center" building first
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires "Manufacturing Center" + "Advanced Research Lab"
- **Nano Factory:** Requires "Quantum Factory" + "Nanobot Manufacturing Center"
- **Dyson Sphere:** Requires "Orbital Elevator Mk3" + "Advanced Research Lab"

**Challenge:** Must build infrastructure chain, can't skip steps.

#### Technology Prerequisites
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires Electronics level 12+ (quantum computing)
- **Nano Factory:** Requires Electronics level 15+ (nanotechnology)
- **Dyson Sphere:** Requires Propulsion level 16+ (space construction)

**Challenge:** Must research multiple tech trees, not just Improvements.

#### Multiple Building Slots
- **Quantum Factory:** Takes 2 building slots (massive facility)
- **Nano Factory:** Takes 3 building slots (planet-scale facility)
- **Dyson Sphere Component:** Takes 1 slot, but need 3-5 components total

**Challenge:** Limits other buildings on the planet.

---

### 5. **Happiness & Social Challenges**

#### Happiness Penalties
- **Advanced Research Lab:** -1 happiness (noise pollution, dangerous experiments)
- **Quantum Factory:** -2 happiness (environmental concerns, radiation fears)
- **Nano Factory:** -3 happiness (massive industrial pollution, health concerns)
- **Dyson Sphere:** -1 happiness per component (massive construction disruption)

**Challenge:** Must balance with happiness buildings or face rebellion.

#### Unhappiness Events
- **Quantum Factory:** 5% chance per turn of "Quantum Accident" event
  - Causes -2 happiness for 5 turns
  - Small chance of destroying building
  
- **Nano Factory:** 3% chance per turn of "Nano Contamination" event
  - Causes -3 happiness for 10 turns
  - Reduces population by 1
  
- **Dyson Sphere:** 2% chance per turn of "Construction Accident" event
  - Delays construction
  - Costs additional resources

**Challenge:** Adds risk/reward element - powerful but dangerous.

---

### 6. **Construction Time & Phases**

#### Multi-Phase Construction
- **Advanced Research Lab:** 2 phases (Foundation + Equipment)
  - Phase 1: 50% cost, provides +1 research
  - Phase 2: 50% cost, upgrades to full +2 research

- **Quantum Factory:** 3 phases (Foundation + Core + Equipment)
  - Each phase requires separate construction
  - Can't skip phases

- **Dyson Sphere:** 5 phases (Anchor Points + Framework + Collectors + Power Transfer + Completion)
  - Each phase is separate building
  - Must complete in order
  - Each phase provides partial benefits

**Challenge:** Requires long-term commitment, can't rush completion.

#### Extended Construction Time
- **Advanced Research Lab:** Takes 3-5 turns to build (vs 1-2 for regular)
- **Quantum Factory:** Takes 5-8 turns to build
- **Nano Factory:** Takes 8-12 turns to build
- **Dyson Sphere Component:** Takes 10-15 turns per component

**Challenge:** Vulnerable during construction - can be destroyed by enemies.

---

### 7. **Planet Condition Requirements**

#### Radiation Requirements
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires Medium-High radiation (needs quantum field)
- **Nano Factory:** Requires Low-Medium radiation (sensitive to high radiation)
- **Dyson Sphere:** Requires Low radiation (solar collectors need clear atmosphere)

**Challenge:** Must find planets with specific radiation levels.

#### Gravity Requirements
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires Standard-High gravity (needs stable environment)
- **Nano Factory:** Requires Standard gravity (precision manufacturing)
- **Dyson Sphere:** Requires Standard gravity (structural integrity)

**Challenge:** Limits planet selection based on gravity tolerance.

#### Stability Requirements
- **Advanced Research Lab:** Planet must be at peace (no recent invasions)
- **Quantum Factory:** Planet must have been owned for 20+ turns (stability)
- **Nano Factory:** Planet must have 0 unhappiness (happy population)
- **Dyson Sphere:** Planet must be in core systems (not border worlds)

**Challenge:** Can't build during war or on unstable planets.

---

### 8. **Strategic Trade-offs**

#### Opportunity Cost
- **Advanced Research Lab:** 
  - Provides +2 research
  - But costs 80 production that could build 4 regular labs (+4 research total)
  - **Trade-off:** Efficiency vs. space efficiency

- **Quantum Factory:**
  - Provides +3 production
  - But costs 120 production (40 turns to pay back)
  - **Trade-off:** Long-term investment

#### Resource Consumption
- **Nano Factory:** Consumes 1 metal per turn (needs rare materials)
- **Dyson Sphere:** Consumes 2 metal per turn per component (maintenance)

**Challenge:** Must ensure steady metal supply.

#### Limited Availability
- **Dyson Sphere:** Only 1 per player (galaxy-wide limit)
- **Nano Factory:** Only 3 per player (galaxy-wide limit)
- **Quantum Factory:** Only 5 per player (galaxy-wide limit)

**Challenge:** Must choose locations carefully - can't spam everywhere.

---

### 9. **Risk & Failure Mechanics**

#### Construction Failure Risk
- **Advanced Research Lab:** 10% chance of failure during construction
  - Loses 50% of invested resources
  - Can retry after 5 turns
  
- **Quantum Factory:** 15% chance of failure
  - Loses 75% of invested resources
  - Can retry after 10 turns
  
- **Nano Factory:** 20% chance of failure
  - Loses all invested resources
  - Can retry after 15 turns
  
- **Dyson Sphere:** 5% chance per component
  - Component destroyed, must rebuild
  - Very expensive to lose!

**Challenge:** Adds gambling element - high risk, high reward.

#### Operational Risks
- **Quantum Factory:** 2% chance per turn of "Quantum Instability"
  - Building disabled for 3 turns
  - Requires repair (costs 20 production)
  
- **Nano Factory:** 1% chance per turn of "Nano Swarm Malfunction"
  - Building destroyed
  - Must rebuild from scratch

**Challenge:** Even after construction, buildings aren't "safe."

---

### 10. **Diplomatic & Military Challenges**

#### Diplomatic Penalties
- **Dyson Sphere:** -10 diplomatic relations with all neighbors
  - "You're blocking our sunlight!"
  - Can cause war declarations
  
- **Nano Factory:** -5 diplomatic relations
  - Environmental concerns
  - Trade embargoes possible

**Challenge:** Powerful buildings make you a target.

#### Military Vulnerability
- **Dyson Sphere Components:** Can be destroyed by orbital bombardment
- **Quantum Factory:** High-value target for enemy spies/sabotage
- **Nano Factory:** Requires strong planetary defense

**Challenge:** Must defend these valuable assets.

#### Espionage Risk
- **Advanced Research Lab:** 5% chance per turn of tech theft
  - Enemy gains random tech
  - Requires counter-espionage buildings
  
- **Quantum Factory:** 3% chance per turn of industrial espionage
  - Enemy gains production bonus
  - Requires security buildings

**Challenge:** Must invest in security infrastructure.

---

### 11. **Special Resource Requirements**

#### Space-Based Components
- **Dyson Sphere:** Requires "Orbital Construction Yard" in same system
  - Must build space station first
  - Station provides construction capability
  
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires "Quantum Stabilizer" component
  - Must research and build special component
  - Component takes 1 ship slot

**Challenge:** Requires space infrastructure, not just planet buildings.

#### Rare Planet Types
- **Quantum Factory:** Only on planets with "Quantum Crystals" resource
  - Rare planet type (5% of planets)
  - Must explore to find
  
- **Nano Factory:** Only on planets with "Nano-Rich Ore" deposits
  - Rare resource (3% of planets)
  - Must mine first

**Challenge:** Limits building locations to rare planets.

---

### 12. **Economic Challenges**

#### Credit Costs
- **Advanced Research Lab:** Requires 50 credits upfront (hiring scientists)
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires 100 credits upfront (specialized equipment)
- **Nano Factory:** Requires 200 credits upfront (licensing fees)
- **Dyson Sphere:** Requires 500 credits per component (space construction)

**Challenge:** Must have strong economy, not just production.

#### Trade Dependencies
- **Quantum Factory:** Requires trade route with 2+ other realms
  - Needs quantum materials from trade
  - Trade embargo disables building
  
- **Nano Factory:** Requires trade route with 3+ other realms
  - Needs rare materials
  - Very vulnerable to trade wars

**Challenge:** Requires diplomatic stability and trade networks.

---

## RECOMMENDED IMPLEMENTATION

### Tier 1: Essential Challenges (Must Have)
1. **High Production/Metal Costs** - Prevents spam
2. **Maintenance Costs** - Ongoing challenge
3. **Planet Size Requirements** - Limits locations
4. **Population Requirements** - Requires development
5. **Building Prerequisites** - Infrastructure chain

### Tier 2: Strategic Challenges (Highly Recommended)
6. **Happiness Penalties** - Social cost
7. **Multi-Phase Construction** - Long-term commitment
8. **Limited Availability** - Strategic choices
9. **Construction Failure Risk** - Risk/reward

### Tier 3: Advanced Challenges (Optional)
10. **Operational Risks** - Ongoing danger
11. **Diplomatic Penalties** - Political cost
12. **Rare Resource Requirements** - Exploration incentive
13. **Trade Dependencies** - Diplomatic requirement

## EXAMPLE: Complete Challenge Set for "Quantum Factory"

### Requirements:
- **Planet Size:** 14+ (very large planet)
- **Population:** 10+ (specialized workforce)
- **Planet Type:** Terrestrial or Desert (stable surface)
- **Radiation:** Medium-High (quantum field requirement)
- **Gravity:** Standard-High (stable environment)
- **Stability:** Owned for 20+ turns (established colony)

### Prerequisites:
- **Buildings:** Manufacturing Center + Advanced Research Lab
- **Technology:** Electronics level 12+ (quantum computing)
- **Resources:** "Quantum Stabilizer" component (must research first)

### Costs:
- **Production:** 120
- **Metal:** 100
- **Credits:** 100 (upfront)
- **Building Slots:** 2 (takes 2 slots)

### Construction:
- **Phases:** 3 phases (Foundation + Core + Equipment)
- **Time:** 5-8 turns total
- **Failure Risk:** 15% chance (loses 75% resources if fails)

### Maintenance:
- **Credits:** 4 per turn
- **Energy:** 5 energy required from planet
- **Population:** 2 population required to operate
- **Metal:** 0.5 metal per turn (rare materials)

### Effects:
- **Production Bonus:** +3 production
- **Happiness:** -2 (environmental concerns)
- **Research Bonus:** +1 (quantum research applications)

### Risks:
- **Operational:** 2% chance per turn of "Quantum Instability" (disabled 3 turns)
- **Espionage:** 3% chance per turn of industrial espionage
- **Diplomatic:** -5 relations with neighbors (environmental concerns)

### Limitations:
- **Availability:** Maximum 5 per player (galaxy-wide)
- **Trade Dependency:** Requires trade route with 2+ realms (quantum materials)

## BALANCING NOTES

### Cost vs. Benefit Analysis
- **Advanced Research Lab:** Costs 80 production, provides +2 research
  - Regular lab costs 20, provides +1 research
  - **Efficiency:** 40 production per research point (vs 20 for regular)
  - **Space Efficiency:** Better (1 slot vs 1 slot, but 2x benefit)
  - **Verdict:** Worth it if you have limited building slots

### Risk vs. Reward
- **Quantum Factory:** High risk (15% failure, operational issues)
  - But provides massive production boost
  - **Verdict:** High risk, high reward - balanced

### Strategic Depth
- **Dyson Sphere:** Requires 5 components, each expensive
  - But provides massive energy and production
  - **Verdict:** Epic project - worth the investment for long games

---

## IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITY

### Phase 1 (Core Challenges):
1. High costs (production, metal, credits)
2. Maintenance costs
3. Planet size/population requirements
4. Building prerequisites

### Phase 2 (Strategic Depth):
5. Happiness penalties
6. Multi-phase construction
7. Limited availability
8. Construction failure risk

### Phase 3 (Advanced Features):
9. Operational risks
10. Diplomatic penalties
11. Rare resource requirements
12. Trade dependencies

This ensures buildings feel **powerful but challenging**, requiring strategic planning and significant investment rather than being "free upgrades"!
